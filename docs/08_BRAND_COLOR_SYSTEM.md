# Kalidass Travels — Brand Color Palette Design System & Print Guidelines

> **Document Version:** 2.0.0  
> **Target Media:** Web, Digital Ads, Social Media, Posters, Billboards, Roll-Up Standees, Vehicle Fleet Decals, In-Cab Kits, Business Stationery  
> **Design Standard:** Google Material Design 3 (M3) Executive Midnight Charcoal & Onyx  
> **Brand Anchors:** Executive Midnight Charcoal (`#1E252D`), Signature Logo "K" Red (`#EC221F`), Deep Midnight Onyx (`#111827`)  
> **Governance:** Zero Red on Tariffs (Rule 2); Standardized WhatsApp CTAs (Rule 8); Minimum WCAG AAA Contrast  

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Brand Philosophy

Kalidass Travels is an executive regional mobility and pilgrimage brand rooted in Chennai, Tamil Nadu. The visual identity projects **quiet confidence, corporate transparency, and immaculate chauffeur reliability**.

Unlike low-cost aggregator cabs that rely on chaotic fluorescent colors and aggressive red sale stickers, the Kalidass Travels brand adheres strictly to the **Executive Midnight Charcoal & Onyx** design system:
1. **Never Scream with Color:** Dignity and trust are established through deep tonal charcoal and calm, anti-glare surfaces.
2. **Prices Are Authoritative, Never Alarming:** Tariffs and rates are always presented in crisp **Deep Midnight Onyx Slate (`#111827`)**. Under no circumstances is price ever printed or displayed in red.
3. **The Logo "K" Monogram is Sacred:** Brand Red (`#EC221F`) is strictly reserved for the signature "K" monogram emblem. It is the single focal point of identity.
4. **Instant Action Through Universal Recognition:** Booking actions utilize **Verified WhatsApp Green (`#25D366`)** and dark charcoal telephone pills, ensuring 100% thumb recognition across South India.

## 3. The 60-30-10 Poster & Billboard Composition Rule

When designing marketing collateral for Kalidass Travels, designers must follow the **60-30-10 rule**:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  60% DOMINANT CANVAS                                                   │

│  30% STRUCTURAL DEPTH & CONTENT                                        │
```

## 6. Strict Brand Guardrails: Do's & Don'ts

```
✅ DO:
• Present tariffs and fares strictly in Deep Midnight Onyx Slate (#111827).
• Reserve Signature Brand Red (#EC221F) exclusively for the "K" logo monogram mark.
• Use Verified WhatsApp Green (#25D366) with crisp white text for booking CTAs and QR code borders.
• Maintain a minimum 60% clean canvas (White, Tone 98 Canvas, or Rich Midnight Black) to avoid visual clutter.
• Pair Plus Jakarta Sans with Noto Sans Tamil for professional bilingual regional balance.
• Use Golden Amber (#D97706) for review star ratings and pilgrimage temple accents.

❌ DON'T:
• NEVER write prices, rates, or "Only ₹600" in Red (#EC221F or #DC2626). This violates Rule 2.
• NEVER use rainbow gradients (e.g. purple-to-pink or blue-to-green) on professional mobility posters.
• NEVER print light grey text (#9CA3AF) on white backgrounds — it fails readability tests outdoors.
• NEVER distort or stretch the "K" logo monogram aspect ratio.
• NEVER use decorative faux-italic scripts or comic fonts for chauffeur and corporate collateral.
• NEVER use plain black (K:100%) for massive dark poster backgrounds; always use Rich Black (C:60 M:40 Y:40 K:100).
```

## 7. Pre-Flight Print Production Checklist

Before sending any poster, standee, or brochure to the print shop:

1. **Color Space Verification:**
   - [ ] All print artwork must be converted to **CMYK** (ISO Coated v2 or US Web Coated SWOP v2).
   - [ ] Spot colors (Pantone PMS 433 C, PMS 1795 C) assigned if using 2-color or 4-color offset printing.
2. **Resolution (DPI):**
   - [ ] Handheld flyers, business cards, A4 posters: **300 DPI** at 100% scale.
   - [ ] Roll-up standees (3×6 ft): **200 DPI** at 100% scale.
   - [ ] Large highway hoardings & billboards (10×20 ft): **100–150 DPI** at 100% scale.
3. **Bleed & Margins:**
   - [ ] Bleed: Add **3 mm (0.125 inches)** outside the cut boundary on all 4 sides.
   - [ ] Safety Margin: Keep all text, logos, and phone numbers at least **5 mm (0.2 inches)** inside the trim line.
4. **Rich Black Formula:**
   - [ ] Small body text ($< 24\text{pt}$): Standard `C:0, M:0, Y:0, K:100` (prevents registration ghosting).
   - [ ] Solid dark backgrounds and black photo backdrops: Rich Black `C:60, M:40, Y:40, K:100`.
5. **Legibility & Contact Verification:**
   - [ ] Phone number `+91 89395 39211` verified and dialed.
   - [ ] QR code scanned with multiple smartphone cameras from 1 meter distance before batch printing.
   - [ ] Website URL `kalidasstravels.in` spelled correctly.
