Kalidass Travels — Booking, Advance & Payment Rules

1. Standard Booking Confirmation Flow
1. Inquiry: Collect Pickup Location, Drop Location, Date & Time, Trip Type (Local / Outstation / Airport), and Vehicle Type.
2. Quotation: Share the exact fare calculation (Base km/package + Driver Bata + mention Tolls/Parking/State Permit at actuals).
3. Advance Payment: Request a booking advance via UPI / QR Code to lock the vehicle and driver.
4. Payment Confirmation: Once UPI screenshot or payment arrives, confirm receipt immediately (e.g., "Received Sir/Mam, your booking is confirmed.").
5. Dispatch: Share Driver Name, Phone Number, and Vehicle Registration Number before pickup.
6. Trip Completion & Balance: Customer pays the remaining balance directly via UPI or cash at trip end (or via corporate invoice for B2B clients).

2. Billing & Extra Charges Policy
- Garage-to-Garage / Point-to-Point: Clarify pickup & drop points upfront.
- Extra Hours / Extra Km: Charged as per the Master Tariff table when local package limits are exceeded.
- Tolls, Parking & Interstate Permits: Billed at actuals (FASTag / Parivahan receipts provided on request), unless booked under an all-inclusive tour package.
- Outstation Minimum Km: Minimum 250 km/day for cars (300 km/day for Tempo Traveller / Urbania) on round trips.
- Night Driving / Stay: Outstation cab driver night stay allowance is ₹450/night when applicable.

3. Quick WhatsApp Reply Templates
- Asking for Trip Details:
  "Sure Sir/Mam! Please share your Pickup Location, Drop Location, Date & Time, and number of passengers so I can give you the exact best rate."
- Requesting Advance:
  "To confirm your cab booking, please pay the advance using our official UPI ID / QR code and share the screenshot here."
- Confirming Advance Receipt:
  "Received Sir/Mam! Thank you. Your booking is confirmed. We will share the driver and vehicle details before pickup."
- Sharing Driver Details:
  "Your cab is assigned! Driver: [Name], Phone: [Number], Vehicle: [Model & Reg No]. Have a safe journey with Kalidass Travels!"
