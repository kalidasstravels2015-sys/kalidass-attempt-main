Kalidass Travels — Driver & Attached Cab Onboarding

1. Attaching a Cab (Vehicle Owners & Fleet Partners)
- Registration Fee: ₹0 (100% Free). Kalidass Travels never charges any upfront registration or onboarding fee.
- Accepted Vehicles: Sedan (Dzire, Etios), SUV (Ertiga), MUV (Innova, Innova Crysta), Tempo Traveller, Force Urbania, Mini Bus.
- Board Types Accepted: Both Yellow Board (commercial) and White Board vehicles are accepted on the onboarding portal.
- Multi-Car Fleet Partners: Owners with 2 or more cars can add all vehicles in a single submission using the "+ Add Another Vehicle" option on the form.

2. How Commission & Trip Settlement Works
- Model: Per-trip outsourced dispatch (not a fixed monthly salary).
- Commission: Standard 10% agency commission on outsourced bookings (90% of the trip fare goes to the vehicle partner).
- Settlement: If the customer pays the trip balance directly to the driver/owner at trip completion, the partner simply settles the net 10% commission difference with the Kalidass Travels dispatch desk.

3. Required Documents & Registration Link
- Mandatory Documents:
  1. Valid Driving License (DL)
  2. Vehicle RC Book
  3. Clear Car Front Photo (showing number plate)
- How to Apply (Do NOT send docs randomly if not yet registered):
  Please submit your details and upload your DL, RC, and Car Front photo directly on our official portal:
  https://kalidasstravels.in/attach-cab/
  (Once submitted, your profile enters our Dispatch Roster verification queue automatically.)

4. Acting Drivers (Without Own Vehicle)
- Experienced, background-verified drivers looking for acting driver / call-driver duties can apply via https://kalidasstravels.in/driver-join/ or contact the dispatch desk at +91 89395 39211.
