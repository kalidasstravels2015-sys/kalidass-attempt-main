2. How to Request a Corporate Quotation (BOQ)

2. Service Type: Monthly dedicated car / Employee shift transport / Ad-hoc outstation or airport drops
3. Vehicle Type & Quantity (e.g., Sedan, Innova Crysta, 14-Seater Tempo Traveller)
4. Daily Route / Approximate Monthly Km & Duty Hours (e.g., 10 hrs / 2,500 km per month)
