# Design System & UI/UX Guidelines — Kalidass Travels

> **Full Document:** Please refer to the comprehensive [docs/04_DESIGN.md](./docs/04_DESIGN.md) and [DESIGN_SYSTEM.md](./DESIGN_SYSTEM.md) for full design system tokens and component specs.

### Design System Highlights
- **Design Language:** Google Material Design 3 (M3).
- **Theme:** Executive Midnight Charcoal (`#1E252D`) + Slate Steel (`#475467`) + Warm Golden Amber (`#6D5E0F`).
- **Typography:** 15-role M3 typescale (`text-m3-display-l` down to `text-m3-label-s`).
- **Surface Ladder:** Tone 98 (`#F9FAFB`) canvas to Tone 100 (`#FFFFFF`) elevated cards and Tone 94 (`#ECEEF2`) form inputs.
- **Mobile UX:** Fixed bottom floating actions (`FloatingActions.astro`), 48x48px touch targets, and virtual keyboard protection via `useVirtualKeyboard`.

See [docs/04_DESIGN.md](./docs/04_DESIGN.md) for complete specifications.
