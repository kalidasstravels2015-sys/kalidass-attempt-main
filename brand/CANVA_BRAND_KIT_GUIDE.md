## 2. Canva Brand Fonts Setup

In Canva, go to **Brand Kit** ➔ **Brand Fonts**:

1. **Title / Headings:**
   - **Font:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` (Available natively in Canva)
   - **Weight:** `ExtraBold (800)` or `Bold (700)`
   - **Color:** `#111827` (Midnight Onyx) or `#FFFFFF` (on dark backgrounds)
2. **Subheadings / Card Headers:**
   - **Font:** `Plus Jakarta Sans`
   - **Weight:** `SemiBold (600)`
   - **Color:** `#1E252D` (Midnight Charcoal)
3. **Body Text & Vehicle Specs:**
   - **Font:** `Plus Jakarta Sans`
   - **Weight:** `Medium (500)` or `Regular (400)`
   - **Color:** `#4B5563` (Muted Slate)
4. **Tamil Regional Bilingual Collateral:**
   - **Font:** `Noto Sans Tamil` (Available natively in Canva)
   - **Weight:** `Bold (700)` for titles, `Medium (500)` for body

## 5. Strict Brand Guardrails in Canva Designs

- ✅ **DO** write all prices, package amounts, and per-km rates in **Deep Midnight Onyx Slate (`#111827`)**.
- ✅ **DO** reserve **Brand Red (`#EC221F`)** exclusively for the signature "K" monogram mark.
- ✅ **DO** use **WhatsApp Green (`#25D366`)** for the primary contact CTA.
- ❌ **DON'T write prices in red (Rule 2 Violation)**: Never write *"Only ₹600"* in `#EC221F` or `#DC2626`.
- ❌ **DON'T use rainbow or neon gradients**: Avoid bright purple, cyan, or multi-color gradients.
- ❌ **DON'T use decorative script or comic fonts**: Maintain executive chauffeur elegance with Plus Jakarta Sans.
